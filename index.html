ABOUT THE PROJECT

Built by a collector, for collectors

Ratchet Legacy started as one fan's mission to catalog a growing Ratchet & Clank collection — and grew into a complete hub for the whole community: a reference database, a wiki, the full story of the saga, and a place to trade with fellow fans.

Everything here is made with love for the series — from the PS2 classics to Rift Apart. No ads, no tracking, just a passion project by and for the people who care about this galaxy.
